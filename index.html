Store Compliance & Review Checklist

Google Play Console Requirements

  • Store Listing Privacy URL: Use the exact app-specific URL: https://madcodelab.github.io/Policy/privacy/zoovoice.html.
  • Developer Name: Verify the developer entity name in Play Console matches exactly: MadCodeLab.
  • Target Audience & Content (Families): Select Children (under 13) and Families. Ensure "Follow Families Policy" is checked.
  • Data Safety Form:
    • Personal Data: None collected or shared.
    • Microphone / Audio: Used strictly on-device for gameplay (Never collected or transferred off-device).
    • Photos / Files: Coloring drawings stored strictly in local device sandbox.
    • AdMob Signals: Device IDs and diagnostics processed transiently for G-rated contextual ads only.
  • Ad Content Rating: Enforce child-directed ad requests (tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true) and maximum rating G.

Apple App Store Connect Requirements

  • Privacy Policy URL: https://madcodelab.github.io/Policy/privacy/zoovoice.html.
  • App Privacy Details: Declare that user audio data is not collected for tracking or identity. Mark all data collection as "Not linked to user identity".
  • Kids Category Guidelines (1.3 & 5.1.4): Ensure Parental Gate is active on all outgoing links and settings. Non-personalized ads only; no third-party tracking or behavioral analytics.
  • Microphone Permission String: Must clearly explain in NSMicrophoneUsageDescription that the microphone is only used for children to imitate animal sounds on-device.
