Privacy Policy for Sky Steps

Last updated November 20, 2026

This policy applies to Sky Steps (com.h2qglobal.skysteps) on iOS and Android. MadCodeLab publishes the game. You can play offline without an account.

Game data stored on your device

Sky Steps saves your best score, coins, number of runs, language, and volume settings on your device so they’re available the next time you play. The game’s own diagnostic logs are stored on your device. Google’s advertising SDK may send its own diagnostic data as described below.

Sharing a score

When you tap the score sharing button on a supported device, Sky Steps copies a message with your score and best score to your clipboard and saves a screenshot in the app’s local storage. You choose where to paste or share the message.

Advertising and privacy choices on iOS

Sky Steps uses Google AdMob on iOS to show ads when the app opens or returns to the foreground, between runs, and when you choose to watch an ad for an in-game reward. Sky Steps requests non-personalized ads and does not ask for permission to access the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA).

Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform may process information such as your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device identifiers available to the SDK, ad views and other interactions, and crash, performance, and other diagnostic data. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, operate consent controls, improve its services, and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where a consent message applies, you can make choices in that message and review or change them in Settings → Ad privacy choices when that option is available. Google handles data it receives under the Google Privacy Policy, including its retention practices.

In-app purchases on iOS

Apple processes payments through the App Store. Sky Steps uses StoreKit to receive verified product and transaction information and check which purchases to unlock or restore. MadCodeLab does not receive your payment card details through the game.

Retention and deletion

To remove local Sky Steps data, use Delete App on iOS or clear the app’s storage in Android settings. If your device backs up app data, you can manage those copies in the platform’s backup settings.

Support and privacy requests

If you email MadCodeLab, we receive your email address and the details you choose to provide. Gmail delivers and stores your message. We use this information to respond and keep it for as long as needed to handle your request or meet legal obligations.

For privacy questions or to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you sent us, email madcodelab68@gmail.com. A parent or guardian can also contact us to request deletion of information a child sent to support.

Updates to this policy

We will post policy updates and the revision date on this page.
