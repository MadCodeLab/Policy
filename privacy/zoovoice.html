1. Policy Availability & Scope

This document is an official, publicly accessible, non-editable HTML privacy policy. Both the Google Play Console store listing, Apple App Store Connect product page, and the in-app settings link directly to this URL.

This policy applies strictly to the Voice Zoo mobile application. If MadCodeLab develops or publishes other applications, each application maintains an independent policy corresponding to its specific functionality.

2. Child-Directed Commitment (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Voice Zoo is designed as an educational, interactive animal discovery experience suitable for young children, preschoolers, and families. We strictly abide by the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR Article 8 for children), Google Play Families Policy, and Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Section 1.3 & 5.1.4 Kids Category).

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do NOT knowingly request, collect, store, or solicit Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from children, such as first/last names, residential addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, precise geolocation, social security numbers, or photographs.
  • No Account Registration: The application functions completely without user accounts, logins, or profile creation.
  • No Public Sharing or Social Networking: Children cannot post content publicly, chat with other users, or transmit data to external social networks.

3. Device Permissions & On-Device Processing

🎙️ Microphone & Audio Processing (Strictly On-Device)

Voice Zoo provides an interactive voice feature where children can imitate animal sounds and practice pronunciation. To support this:

  • The app requests runtime microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO on Android and NSMicrophoneUsageDescription on iOS).
  • Audio data is processed entirely locally on the user’s device.
  • Audio recordings are NEVER uploaded, streamed, or saved to external servers, cloud services, or third-party platforms.
  • We do NOT use voice recordings for biometric identification, voice recognition modeling, or advertising targeting.
  • Recordings are discarded immediately after play or stored only in private temporary local device storage that the parent can clear at any time.

Local Storage (Artworks & Progress)

The app includes an animal coloring book and educational quizzes. Children's colored artworks, earned star badges, and sound settings (BGM/SFX toggles, English/Vietnamese language choices) are saved strictly inside the device’s local sandbox (via SharedPreferences / local filesystem). This data remains on the device and is never synchronized to external servers. Clearing application data or uninstalling the app permanently deletes this local content.

Push Notifications

The app declares notification permissions (POST_NOTIFICATIONS on Android 13+) solely for optional, friendly local educational reminders. We do not transmit advertising campaigns or spam via push notifications. Parents may disable notifications anytime via the operating system settings.

4. Advertising Practices (Google AdMob in Families Policy)

Where advertising is enabled, Voice Zoo integrates the official Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). MadCodeLab enforces the strictest family-safe configurations:

  • Child-Directed Treatment: Every ad request is explicitly flagged with tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true and tagForUnderAgeOfConsent = true.
  • Maximum Content Rating G: We enforce maxAdContentRating = G so that all served ads are strictly suitable for general audiences and preschool/primary school children.
  • Non-Personalized Ads Only: Behavioral advertising, retargeting, interest-based ad profiling, and cross-app tracking are strictly prohibited and disabled.
  • No Identifier Tracking: The app does not utilize IDFA or Advertising ID for tracking children across apps or websites.

Data processed by Google AdMob is limited to technical signals necessary for ad delivery, contextual ad rendering, fraud detection, frequency capping, and aggregated delivery metrics under Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads Data Disclosure.

5. Parental Gate (Apple Guideline 1.3 & Google Families)

To ensure that young children remain safely inside the educational environment, Voice Zoo incorporates a robust Parental Gate. Any action that navigates outside the core gameplay—including accessing developer settings, support links, or external store review pages—requires solving an adult-directed mathematical verification prompt. This prevents accidental navigation or unintended interaction with external websites.

7. Third-Party Services

The app may communicate with trusted third-party SDKs strictly for operational functionality:

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob: For delivering G-rated contextual ads, app integrity, and distribution (Google Policies).
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC): For delivering optional educational reminders.
  • MadCodeLab Infrastructure: Backend services (api.madcodelab.cloud) protected by HMAC security signatures, used solely to verify app remote configurations and system health without processing user data.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Because MadCodeLab does not host child user accounts or maintain remote databases containing personal user information, we hold no user data to delete from our servers. All gameplay achievements, artwork, and settings are retained solely on the local device. Parents can permanently erase all stored data at any time by:

  1. Clearing the app storage/cache in Android Settings (Settings → Apps → Voice Zoo → Storage → Clear Data).
  2. Deleting and reinstalling the app on iOS or Android.
