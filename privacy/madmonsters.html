Privacy Policy for MadMonsters

Last updated November 19, 2026

MadCodeLab operates MadMonsters (com.h2qglobal.madmonsters) and its online game services for iOS and Android. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can manage or delete it.

Account and gameplay data

  • Account information. We collect your email address, chosen Trainer name, and account and Player IDs. We process your password, session tokens, and verification or recovery information to create and secure your account, sign you in, and help you recover access. The app stores session information and preferences such as language and sound on your device.
  • Gameplay and social activity. Our game servers store your profile, avatar selection, monster roster, teams, progression, inventory, virtual currency balances, purchases made with in-game currency, battle and event results, rankings, Friend activity, and Guild activity. We use these records to save your progress, deliver game features, and enforce fair play.
  • Information other players can see. Player search, Friend lists, multiplayer battles, rankings, and Guild listings or rosters can show your Trainer name, Player ID, selected avatar, level, scores, battle lineups, and Guild details such as the Guild name and description. Keep personal contact details out of fields visible to other players.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple and Google handle payment details through App Store In-App Purchase and Google Play Billing. When you buy a pack or a subscription where offered, MadMonsters uses store data such as the product ID, transaction ID, receipt or purchase token, and purchase status to validate transactions, deliver benefits, handle refunds or disputes, and prevent duplicate grants and fraud. We record the purchase and the benefits delivered to your game account.

Store operators handle billing information under their own policies. See the Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics, operational alerts, and diagnostics

  • PostHog. The app sends events to our PostHog analytics service when you open or use the app, view screens, sign in or register, summon monsters, play battles, make purchase attempts, or encounter errors. Events include timestamps, app version, device model, operating system, language, and screen size. The app assigns an analytics identifier and can associate it with your account ID, email address, and Trainer name. Purchase and error events can include product and transaction IDs, results, and error details. We use this information to understand game usage and investigate problems.
  • Operational alerts via Telegram. The app sends events to our operational alerting service for Telegram alerts. Alerts can include your email address and account ID for sign-in or registration, part of a device identifier and device or app information, purchase product and transaction IDs, and API or runtime error details. We use alerts to monitor service availability, investigate account or purchase problems, and respond to failures. Telegram Privacy Policy
  • Firebase on iOS. Google Analytics for Firebase can collect app interactions, session data, app-instance identifiers, device and app information, and general location inferred from IP addresses to measure app use. Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports, which can include device and operating system details, stack traces, and app activity leading up to a crash to help us identify and fix errors. Privacy and security in Firebase
  • Connections and security. Our services process IP addresses, connection and request metadata, and error information to deliver and troubleshoot the game and protect the service against abuse. The game uses HTTPS to communicate with our game servers.

Push notifications on iOS

Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service can process an app installation ID, device registration tokens, device and app details, and interactions with notifications to deliver messages and measure delivery or engagement. You can manage visible notifications under Settings → Notifications → MadMonsters on your device. Messaging registration and token generation can continue while visible alerts are disabled.

Providers and disclosure

We use providers for hosting, database operations, account verification and recovery email, analytics, alerts, crash reporting, notifications, and store billing. We require providers processing personal data on our behalf to protect it to at least the standard described in this policy and use it for the services they provide to us.

We limit our use of the data described here to running and improving MadMonsters, supporting players, protecting accounts and the service, and meeting legal obligations. We may disclose information where legally required or to address fraud, security issues, or legal claims. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to applicable data protection requirements.

When you contact us, we use the email address, message, and any information you choose to provide to respond, verify your request where necessary, and resolve your issue.

Retention and account deletion

We keep account and gameplay data to provide the service while your account is active. We retain analytics, diagnostics, and support records as needed to operate the service, investigate issues, and respond to requests. After deletion, we may retain limited transaction, refund, or security records as long as necessary to meet legal and accounting obligations, resolve disputes, or prevent fraud or abuse.

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data from Celestial Settings → Trainer Profile Dossier → Delete account or through our account deletion page. Once game account deletion is complete, you lose access to your Trainer profile, progress, and virtual items. The deletion page explains how to contact us about the status of your request and how to manage an active store subscription.

Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you can request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your personal data, object to or request restriction of certain uses, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. To exercise these rights or ask about a retention period, contact madcodelab68@gmail.com. We may ask for information to verify the request. Where applicable, you can also contact your local data protection authority.

Children’s privacy

MadMonsters is intended for teens and older players. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information without any consent required by law, you can contact us to request its deletion at madcodelab68@gmail.com.

Policy updates and contact

We will post changes with a revised date on this page. For privacy questions or requests, contact MadCodeLab at madcodelab68@gmail.com.
