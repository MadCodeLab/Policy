1. Policy Scope & Availability

This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to the MadMonsters mobile game client, its official authentication links (madmonster://auth/), and its authoritative backend services operated by MadCodeLab. This document is hosted at a publicly accessible, non-editable URL linked directly from our Google Play Store listing, Apple App Store product page, and developer portal.

2. Information We Collect & Process

MadMonsters adheres strictly to the principle of data minimization. We only collect and process data essential to authenticate your trainer account, synchronize your cross-session RPG progression, facilitate structured multiplayer features, and protect game integrity.

A. Account & Authentication Data

  • Email Address: Collected when you register a MadMonsters account so you can log in across sessions, verify account ownership via confirmation links, and recover forgotten passwords via one-time recovery codes (OTP) or reset links.
  • Trainer Display Name: A fictional in-game nickname chosen by you to represent your Trainer profile in battles, Arena rankings, Friends lists, and Guild rosters. You are not required or encouraged to use your real-world legal name.
  • Cryptographic Password Protection: Passwords are never stored in plain text. All credentials are cryptographically salted and hashed using modern memory-hard algorithms (Argon2id / Supabase Auth identity protection). Neither our staff nor third parties can read your password.
  • Internal Account Identifier: A randomly generated UUID (user_id) and numerical Trainer ID assigned by our server to associate your gameplay state with your account.

B. Authoritative Gameplay & Progression State

Because MadMonsters is an online tactical RPG with server-authoritative anti-cheat validation, we store your game state on our secure database servers, including:

  • Monster Roster & Training Records: Owned creatures, species codex unlocks, levels, star ascensions, potential allocations, learned skills, skill tiers, rebirth history, and squad formations.
  • Inventory & Virtual Currencies: In-game balances (such as Gold and Diamonds), skill books, upgrade stones, stamina, magic energy, quest progress, and shop exchange logs.
  • Combat & Event Records: Campaign stage completions, Trial Tower floors, Spacetime Rifts, Island Expeditions, Arena defensive/offensive battle logs, World Boss raid damage rankings, Friend relationships, Friendly Duel replays, and Guild membership/Guild War standings.

C. Financial & In-App Purchase (IAP) Verification

Where optional in-app purchases (such as virtual currency packs or Privilege Passes) are offered, all payment transactions are processed directly by Google Play Billing (on Android) or Apple StoreKit / In-App Purchase (on iOS).

  • Zero Payment Card Access: MadCodeLab never collects, receives, processes, or stores your credit card numbers, debit card details, bank account numbers, or billing addresses.
  • Receipt Validation Only: Our servers only receive cryptographic transaction receipts, order IDs, and product SKU identifiers from Google or Apple to verify purchase authenticity, grant virtual items, prevent duplicate fulfillment, and reconcile refunds.

D. Technical Security, Network & Diagnostic Logs

  • Connection Metadata & Anti-Abuse Signals: When the game communicates with api-madmonsters.madcodelab.cloud, our security gateway processes your IP address transiently, request timestamps, single-use cryptographic nonces, and HMAC-SHA256 request signatures strictly for DDoS mitigation, rate limiting, and anti-tamper replay protection.
  • Operational Diagnostics: Aggregated server latency metrics and sanitized error logs (with automatic redaction of tokens, passwords, and secrets) are recorded to maintain server uptime and fix gameplay bugs.

E. Local On-Device Storage

  • Session Credentials & Preferences: Encrypted/signed JWT access and refresh tokens (madmonster_auth_session.json), audio volume levels (BGM/SFX), and language selection (English, Vietnamese, Simplified Chinese) are saved solely inside the operating system's private application sandbox on your device.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect & Device Permissions

Strict Permission Minimization: MadMonsters requests only standard network access (android.permission.INTERNET) required to connect to the authoritative game server.

The application DOES NOT request access to, and never collects data from, your Camera, Microphone, Photo Gallery, Contacts/Address Book, SMS/Call Logs, Calendar, Health Sensors, or Precise GPS Location.

  • No Behavioral Ad Tracking: MadMonsters does not sell your personal information to data brokers, does not track you across third-party apps or websites (no IDFA/GAID cross-app profiling), and does not use your account email for unsolicited third-party marketing.

4. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR / UK GDPR / International Laws)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Vietnam, or another jurisdiction requiring a lawful basis for processing personal data, we process your information on the following legal grounds:

  • Performance of a Contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): Processing your account credentials, gameplay progression, social/guild interactions, and purchase receipts is necessary to provide the MadMonsters game services you request.
  • Legitimate Interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): Processing technical security logs, IP rate limits, and server-authoritative combat verification is necessary to safeguard infrastructure security, prevent cheating, and maintain fair leaderboards.
  • Legal Obligations (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)): Retaining minimal non-personal transaction audit records where required by applicable tax, accounting, or consumer protection regulations.
  • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)): Where required by local law for specific optional actions, which you may withdraw at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.

6. Public In-Game Identifiers & Community Safety

MadMonsters uses structured, server-validated social interactions (Friend requests by Player ID, stamina gifting, Duel challenges, and Guild Wars) and does not include open, unmoderated private text chat, photo sharing, or voice chat between strangers.

However, because Trainer Display Names and Guild Names are visible to other players on Arena leaderboards and Guild listings, we enforce strict naming standards in accordance with Apple App Store Guideline 1.2 and Google Play User-Generated Content Policies:

  • Names containing profanity, hate speech, sexual content, harassment, impersonation, or unlawful material are strictly prohibited.
  • Players may report inappropriate Trainer or Guild names directly to madcodelab68@gmail.com. Violating names are forcibly reset or permanently suspended within 24 hours of verification.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share limited data solely with essential infrastructure providers acting as data processors under strict confidentiality and security obligations:

  • Cloud Hosting & Database Infrastructure (Oracle Cloud Infrastructure / Self-Hosted Supabase Auth & PostgreSQL): Hosts our authoritative Go game servers, encrypted authentication records, and transactional SMTP confirmation/recovery emails.
  • Platform Store Operators (Google LLC & Apple Inc.): Process app distribution and, where applicable, platform-native In-App Purchase verification under their respective privacy policies.
  • Legal Disclosures: We may disclose information only if strictly required by valid legal process, court order, or governmental authority to protect user safety or legal rights.

International Data Transfers: Because MadMonsters operates globally, your gameplay and account data may be processed on secure cloud servers located outside your state or country of residence. We implement standard encryption and contractual safeguards to ensure your data remains protected wherever processed.

8. Data Security Measures

  • Encryption in Transit: 100% of HTTP API calls and real-time WebSocket (`WSS`) connections are encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS 1.3.
  • Cryptographic Password & Token Security: Passwords are protected with salted Argon2id hashing; sessions use short-lived signed JWT access tokens paired with revocable refresh tokens.
  • Request Integrity & Rate Limiting: Dynamic endpoint security policies, HMAC-SHA256 verification, and automated IP abuse protection guard against unauthorized access and credential stuffing.
  • Access Control & Secret Redaction: Production databases operate inside firewalled private networks with least-privilege roles and automated log redaction for sensitive fields.

9. Data Retention & Account Deletion Rights

We retain your account and progression data only for as long as your MadMonsters account remains active. In full compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy and Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), you may permanently delete your account and associated personal data at any time:

  • API / In-App Account Deletion: Authenticated accounts support direct server-side deletion via our account deletion endpoint, immediately revoking active sessions and erasing your account profile.
  • Web / Out-of-App Deletion Request (No App Install Required): If you have uninstalled MadMonsters or cannot log in, visit our dedicated MadMonsters Account & Data Deletion Page or send an email from your registered address to madcodelab68@gmail.com.
  • What Is Deleted & Timeline: Upon verified request, your email address, authentication credentials, Trainer profile, monster collection, inventory, Friend links, and gameplay history are permanently purged within 7 to 30 days. Short-lived automated security/rate-limit logs expire automatically within 30–90 days, and anonymized financial tax/audit records (if any IAP occurred) are retained solely as required by statutory accounting laws.
