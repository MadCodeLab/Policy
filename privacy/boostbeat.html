Privacy Policy for BoostBeat

Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy explains how MadCodeLab handles information in BoostBeat (com.h2qglobal.boostbeat) on iOS and Android. You can play without a BoostBeat account.

Gameplay and settings

BoostBeat keeps your game progress on your device, including tutorial completion, campaign progress, high scores, and coins. It also saves your language and volume preferences. In versions with a lives system, the game saves your life balance and refill time on your device so they are available when you return.

Advertising and privacy choices

In versions with ads enabled, Google AdMob can show ads while the game loads and offer optional ads for in-game rewards. When active, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad views and other app or ad interactions, and crash or performance diagnostics. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, improve ad performance, and prevent fraud. Ad personalization depends on the configuration, your consent, and your device permissions.

BoostBeat may save recent ad display times on your device to limit how often loading-screen ads appear.

Where available, open Privacy Choices in the game settings to review, change, or withdraw your consent for future ad requests. You can manage tracking permissions in iOS settings and advertising identifiers in Android settings.

See how Google handles information in its Privacy Policy and its guide to data from apps that use Google services.

In-app purchases

In versions with in-app purchases, Apple or Google handles billing and payment details through its store. BoostBeat uses information supplied by the store, such as the product ID, transaction ID, and purchase status, to verify purchases, deliver items once per transaction, and restore eligible purchases. The game may keep transaction identifiers and purchase entitlements on your device for these purposes.

The stores explain how they handle payment and account information in the Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Retention, deletion, and support

BoostBeat keeps its save in the app’s private storage on your device. To remove the local save, delete the app on iOS or clear its app storage on Android. The platform or ad provider manages device backups, store purchase history, and ad data under its own policies. We require third parties we share user data with to provide protections consistent with this policy and the applicable store requirements.

If you email MadCodeLab, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and retain it as long as needed to handle your request or meet legal obligations. To ask about privacy or request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold, email madcodelab68@gmail.com.

Children’s privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to MadCodeLab, you can request its deletion by contacting us at madcodelab68@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We will post any updates to this policy and its revision date on this page.
