Privacy Policy for Dual Lane Switcher

Last updated November 19, 2026

MadCodeLab publishes Dual Lane Switcher on iOS (com.h2qglobal.duallaneswitcher) and Android (com.game.duallaneswitcher). You can play without creating a game account.

Game saves and settings

The game saves your best score and survival time, tutorial progress, diamonds, vehicle selections and unlocks, and highest evolution level. It also saves your language, audio, vibration, and control settings. This information stays in the app’s private storage on your device so you can continue where you left off.

Advertising and consent

Google AdMob serves full-screen ads that may appear on some retries or when you return to the game. You can also choose to watch a rewarded video to revive. The game keeps a launch count and the time it last showed an App Open ad on your device to limit how often those ads appear.

Google’s advertising SDK may collect your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device identifiers and advertising identifiers where permitted, app and ad interactions such as video views, and crash and performance diagnostics. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, assess performance, and prevent fraud. Ad personalization depends on the ad settings, your consent, and your device permissions.

When Google requires a consent decision, its User Messaging Platform presents a privacy message in the game. Where available, you can use iOS Settings to manage tracking permissions and Android Settings to manage your advertising identifier. To ask for help changing or withdrawing ad consent, contact us using the email below.

Read the Google Privacy Policy and learn how Google handles data from apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

On iOS, Apple handles payment and store account information when you buy the one-time Remove forced ads upgrade. The game uses information from StoreKit, such as the product ID, transaction ID, and purchase status, to verify, apply, and restore ownership on your device.

The game can cache ownership in the device’s Keychain for offline use. The cache contains the store, product ID, purchase environment, and time of the verification. See the Apple Privacy Policy for how Apple handles store data.

Retention and deletion

To erase game progress, settings, and local records used to limit ad frequency, choose Delete App on iOS or clear the game’s app storage on Android. The iOS Keychain purchase cache may persist after deletion. If you reinstall, the game checks ownership with the App Store and updates or clears that cache based on the store’s response. Store purchase history, device backups, and advertising records are handled under the respective providers’ policies.

We require third parties we share user data with to provide privacy protections consistent with this policy and applicable store requirements.

Support and privacy requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to handle your request and keep it as long as needed to resolve the request or meet legal obligations.

For privacy questions or to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information MadCodeLab holds, email madcodelab68@gmail.com. Parents and guardians can also contact us about information their child has shared with us.
