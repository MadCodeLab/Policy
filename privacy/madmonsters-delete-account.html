1. How to Request Account Deletion

Option A: Request Deletion via Web / Email (Without Opening the App)

  1. Send an email from the registered email address associated with your MadMonsters account to: madcodelab68@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: MadMonsters Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your registered email address (and optionally your Trainer Name or Player ID for faster lookup).
  4. Because the request originates from your verified email address, no additional password or sensitive document is required. Our support team will confirm receipt within 48 hours and complete permanent erasure within 7 to 30 days.

2. Data Deletion & Retention Breakdown

When your MadMonsters account is deleted, data is handled strictly according to the schedule below:

Permanently Deleted Data
  • Account Credentials: Registered email address, password hashes, active JWT sessions, and recovery tokens.
  • Trainer Profile & Progress: Trainer Name, Player ID profile, owned Monsters, levels, star ascensions, skills, inventory items, Gold, and Diamonds.
  • Multiplayer & Social Records: Arena defense squads, Friend connections, pending Duel records, and Guild membership links.
Timeline: Permanently and irreversibly purged from active production databases within 7 to 30 days of verification.
Temporarily Retained Data (Security & Legal Compliance)
  • Automated Security & Anti-Abuse Logs: Transient IP rate-limit counters and request integrity logs are automatically rotated and purged within 30 to 90 days.
  • Statutory Accounting Records (If IAP Occurred): Non-personal platform receipt transaction IDs (containing no name or email) are retained solely if required by applicable tax, audit, and financial fraud-prevention laws.
Local Device Cache Uninstalling the MadMonsters app from your Android or iOS device immediately removes all local session files and audio/language settings stored on your device.

Important Warning — Irreversible Action: Account deletion is permanent. Once completed, your Monsters, virtual currencies, campaign progress, and any unlocked in-game items cannot be restored.
