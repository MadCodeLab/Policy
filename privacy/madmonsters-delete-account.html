Delete your MadMonsters account

MadCodeLab • com.h2qglobal.madmonsters • Updated November 19, 2026

You can request permanent deletion of your MadMonsters account and associated personal data by email or from the game.

Request deletion by email

Email an account deletion request

Send your request to madcodelab68@gmail.com using the subject MadMonsters Account Deletion Request. Include the email registered to your account and, if available, your Trainer name or Player ID.

Send the message from your registered email address if you can. If you have lost access to it, explain this in your request so support can help verify that the account is yours. Keep your password, verification codes, and payment details private. You can reply to the request email to check its status.

Delete your account in the game

  1. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Celestial Settings → Trainer Profile Dossier and select Delete account.
  3. Read the confirmation prompt and confirm deletion. The game signs you out after the server confirms the request.

What deletion covers

Account deletion covers your login information, Trainer profile, monsters, progress, inventory, virtual currency, and other personal data associated with your account. Deletion is permanent and you will lose access to game items and account benefits.

MadCodeLab may retain limited records, such as purchase and refund records or security logs, for as long as required for legal or accounting obligations, dispute resolution, and fraud or abuse prevention. Apple and Google manage their own purchase records. For details about retention or a specific request, see the privacy policy or contact support at madcodelab68@gmail.com.

Active subscriptions

Billing for an auto-renewing subscription can continue after account deletion until you cancel through the store that billed you. You can cancel renewal separately and request account deletion at any time. Manage Apple subscriptions · Manage Google Play subscriptions
