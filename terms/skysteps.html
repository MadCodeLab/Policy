Terms of Service for Sky Steps

Last updated November 20, 2026

These terms apply to Sky Steps (com.h2qglobal.skysteps), a two-lane platform game published by MadCodeLab for iOS and Android.

Playing and saving progress

Sky Steps is free to play. You earn coins by playing. Coins are part of your local game progress and have no cash value. Clearing the app’s data, deleting the app, or switching devices may cause you to lose your progress. Recovery depends on your device’s backup and restore options.

Advertising and purchases on iOS

The iOS version may show app-open and between-run ads. You can choose to watch rewarded ads when they are available. The one-time Remove Automatic Ads purchase turns off app-open and between-run ads, while rewarded ads remain optional. The one-time Double Notes purchase doubles the Notes earned from a run.

Apple processes these purchases through the App Store. You can restore eligible purchases using Restore Purchases with the same App Store account. Restoring purchases does not restore local game progress.

Licenses and consumer rights

For App Store downloads, the license listed in the store applies. The Apple Standard EULA applies where no separate license is listed. Open-source and other third-party licenses continue to apply to the materials they cover. Your rights under applicable consumer law continue to apply.
