Ads and rewarded revives

Full-screen ads may appear when you retry or return to the game. When a rewarded video is available, you can choose to watch it to revive. The game grants the revive when the ad provider confirms you earned the reward.

Remove forced ads and restore purchases

Where available on iOS, Remove forced ads is a one-time purchase that disables interstitial ads when you retry and App Open ads when you return to the game. After purchase, you can still choose to watch a rewarded revive video when one is available.

The App Store shows the price before you confirm and processes payment. If you reinstall the game or switch Apple devices, open Settings → Remove forced ads → Restore purchase and use the Apple Account that made the purchase. You can request a refund through Apple, subject to Apple’s terms and applicable law.
